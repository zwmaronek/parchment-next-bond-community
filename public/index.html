How this works

Pick a topic, start a thread, or add a comment to an existing thread. Each thread shows a neutral summary: main points, where commenters seem to agree, where they differ, and open questions. The summary does not take a side.

  • Focus on ideas, not people.
  • No personal attacks and no personal information about anyone.
  • Use the report button if a post breaks these guidelines.

Build a Bond Workshop

Not just open chat. Work through the last three bonds, pick or build the sections that should be in the next one, vote on them, and turn the top ones into a draft the community can see and vote on.

Step 1: What happened last time

Three bonds, three no votes. The 2026 plan was the biggest ask, and the largest share went to North Elementary, then the high school wing.

2026 tax figures, from the ballot: 3.95 mills in 2027 ($395 a year on $100,000 of taxable value), 5.51-mill simple average over 30 years. Existing debt at the time: $15.28 million in bonds and about $10.19 million in loans. Bond money cannot pay salaries or operating costs.

Step 2: Pick your sections and vote

Vote for the sections you would include. Each section keeps its own points: ideas, pros, cons, and open questions, so the reasoning stays with the section instead of getting lost in a long thread.

Step 4: Your draft bond

Your draft is a table, not a pile of paragraphs. Edit any cell: rename a section, move it to another building, or change its cost. Edits change only your draft, not the shared sections, and the total and tax estimate update as you type.
